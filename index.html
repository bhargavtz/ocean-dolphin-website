Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about student visas and overseas education from Surat.

Can I study in the UK without IELTS?

Yes! Many UK universities accept students based on their 12th Grade English score (typically 65% to 70%+ in CBSE/GSEB) or with a Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificate from your previous college. Our counselors will shortlist matching universities for you.

What are the post-study work visa rights in Canada and Australia?

Graduates from recognized Canadian public institutions are eligible for up to 3 years of Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP). In Australia, international graduates can work for 2 to 4 years post-study depending on qualification and regional location.

How much does Ocean Dolphin charge for counseling?

Initial profile assessment and university counseling at Ocean Dolphin are completely FREE. We are official representatives of partner universities across UK, Canada, USA, and Australia.

Do you provide education loan support in Surat?

Yes! We have direct tie-ups with nationalized banks (SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB) and leading NBFCs for both collateral and non-collateral education loans with fast sanction letter issuance.
